Single-family homes are selling a lot faster than condos across the metro — about 2.5 months of inventory for houses versus roughly 6.9 months for condos. In Northeast, North and Southeast Portland single-family listings often move in under a month — Northeast single-family averages about 1.5 months of inventory and 28 days on market. Meanwhile, North Portland condos have nearly 10 months of inventory and take around 112 days to sell. That means buyers chasing houses in those inner neighborhoods need to be ready to act quickly, while condo buyers usually get more time and choices.
