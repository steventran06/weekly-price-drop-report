Single-family homes and condos are moving like two different markets right now. Across Greater Portland single-family inventory is about 2.5 months and average sold homes are on the market around 43 days, which is a faster pace. Contrast that with condos, which sit near 6.9 months of inventory and about 85 days on market, meaning more choices and more time for buyers. Look at North Portland as a dramatic example: single-family homes move in about a month, while condos can take well over three months. If you are buying a detached home in hot neighborhoods, expect quick decisions. If you are shopping condos, expect a slower pace and more room to negotiate.
