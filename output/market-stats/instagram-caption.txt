Single-family vs condos: two very different markets right now.

Single-family (metro): roughly 2.5 months of inventory, average sale about 43 days.
Condominiums (metro): about 6.9 months of inventory, average sale about 85 days.

Neighborhood highlights: Northeast Portland single-family is extremely fast (about 1.5 months of inventory, ~28 days). At the other end, North Portland condos sit near 9.8 months of inventory and ~112 days on market.

If you’re buying, decide whether you need speed or choice. If you’re selling, your prep and timeline should match the local market for your property type.
